FIELDHQ unit staff manual

A guide for unit staff using the roster, personnel records, events and attendance tools. You do not need hosting access to do unit work.

For server administration, see the full user manual. For help with your login or permissions, contact your unit administrator or panel administrator.

1. Getting started

  1. Open the panel address supplied by your administrator.
  2. Sign in using your panel username and password. Staff use the normal panel login page.
  3. Unit-only staff land in Units & rosters. If you also have hosting access, choose Units or Units & rosters in the navigation.
  4. Select your unit. If you belong to several units, check the selected unit before editing anything.
  5. Choose a workspace area, then its page.

A panel account is your login. A roster record is a person in a unit. Most roster members do not need panel accounts.

Use Account settings to change your password or color theme. Your theme changes your colors, not the unit's branding or page layout. Original also gives panels and controls square corners and firmer borders.

Help while you work

Choose Help in the panel header for guidance on the current unit page. Follow the links to read the full unit staff manual inside the panel, search its text, or download the PDF. Help does not discard edits in your current form.

2. Your access and workspace

Ordinary staff see only their assigned units. Panel administrators have broader access. There are three unit roles:

Role Access
Staff Manage rosters, notes, assignments, qualifications, promotion reviews, events, attendance, SOPs/loadouts, awards and campaigns in assigned units
Member Read their unit roster and published resources, acknowledge reading, RSVP and view their own profile; no staff notes or hosting controls
Unit administrator All Staff functions, plus staff access, rank and qualification setup, structure, branding and permanent member deletion

There are no individual permission checkboxes. Existing Staff assignments keep the standard Staff permissions after the update. A unit role does not grant permission to start, stop, configure or update game servers; hosting access is assigned separately.

Where to find things

Workspace area Pages
Overview Dashboard, Analytics
Roster Roster, Qualifications, Promotions, Awards
Operations Events, Attendance, Campaigns, Linked servers
Resources SOPs & loadouts, Rank & qualification setup
Administration Structure, Accounts & access, Customization; available when permitted

The unit Dashboard summarizes staffing, upcoming events and work awaiting review. Analytics summarizes recorded trends. Linked servers provides basic health for the unit's servers; opening their hosting workspace needs separate access.

If a button is missing or disabled, ask an administrator to check your unit role. Linking a server to the unit or linking your account to a member does not grant that permission.

Workspace navigation

Unit pages use the same grouped sidebar pattern as server workspaces. Choose a unit at the top of the sidebar; permitted pages are always visible under group headings. On phones, use Menu to open navigation. Hosting and Units switch workspaces in the top bar; Help and account controls are on the right. All Servers is a full-width landing page without a server sidebar. Its attention strip shows confirmed server errors, unavailable status and low panel-disk space, with links to the affected server or Storage; it is hidden when clear. Panel administrators can open Panel users directly from the landing page.

Member home and self-service

Members use the same /login page as staff. Unit Staff can create a linked Member account using Create login on an active roster record. Unit administrators can also create accounts under Administration → Accounts & access. Members land on Overview → My unit. Staff can open the same page to manage announcements, see document review reminders, and inspect event RSVPs.

  • Required reading: staff open an SOP and choose Reading & review. Assign everyone with a unit login, selected positions / primary billet names, selected qualifications, or a combination. Audiences are combined as “matches any.” A reading deadline is optional. Position and qualification targeting requires an active linked roster record; “everyone” includes unlinked logins. The progress list shows who has acknowledged the current published version.
  • Acknowledging a document: open it from My unit → Required reading, read the published copy, then choose I have read this version. This records a personal acknowledgement, not proof of comprehension. Republishing creates a new version and requires a fresh acknowledgement. An old open document cannot acknowledge a newer publication.
  • SOP review reminders: in Reading & review, assign an active unit staff owner, last-reviewed date and next-review date. Staff see due and overdue reviews on My unit. Review dates are reminders and never automatically unpublish or change a document. After reviewing, update the dates; publish separately if the content changed.
  • Announcements: Staff use My unit → New announcement. Enter a title and message, optionally pin it or set a show-through date. Manage latest 100 announcements includes expired and archived entries for editing or restoration. These are messages inside the panel; no Discord or email delivery is involved.
  • RSVPs: under upcoming events, choose Attending, Tentative or Unavailable, or clear your response. An active roster link is required. Responses close when the event starts. Staff see names and totals; ordinary members see only their own response. Editing an event invalidates responses to the previous event version, so members must respond again. An RSVP never changes UUID attendance, attendance expectations, LOA or an excuse decision. Upcoming event briefings and locations are visible to members.
  • My profile: members see their own game name, Discord handle, status, rank, primary billet, join date, last promotion date, qualification award dates and the latest 100 finalized tracked attendance results. The displayed attendance rate includes expected Present / Absent decisions only. Staff notes, qualification notes, attendance reasons, UUID evidence and administrative history are excluded. Ask staff for corrections; this page does not edit the roster.

Published reading and announcements work without a roster link. Personal profiles, targeted reading and RSVPs require the login-to-member link. Unit and hosting permissions remain separate.

3. Daily staff workflow

Before an event

  1. Select the correct unit.
  2. Check the event's server, start time, timezone, duration and attendance tracking.
  3. Confirm new members have their correct recorded UUID linked.
  4. Review Active, Reserve and LOA statuses.
  5. Coordinate server readiness with hosting staff if you do not have server access.

After an event

  1. Open the event and review attendance evidence and any collection gaps.
  2. Make corrections with reasons.
  3. Finalize the event after it has ended, when permitted.
  4. Record earned qualifications, personnel notes or award issues as appropriate.
  5. Review promotion eligibility; eligibility does not itself promote anyone.

Save each form and wait for success before closing or switching units. If the panel reports that data changed, refresh and review the latest records rather than trying to overwrite another staff member's work.

4. Roster and member records

Find a member

Open Roster → Roster. Search and use the available filters to find the person. A unit may show a flat member list, organized billets, or both views. Groups and ranks are optional.

Open the member's record for personnel details, dated notes, qualifications, awards, attendance and the recorded change timeline.

Add or edit a member

With roster permission:

  1. Add a roster member, or open an existing member's editor.
  2. Enter the game name and optional Discord handle.
  3. Select Active, Reserve or LOA.
  4. Enter the joined date and, if used, rank structure, rank, last-promotion date and primary billet.
  5. Assign qualifications and save.

A person can belong to multiple units with different names, ranks, qualifications and statuses. Updating this unit's record does not update their other units.

Link their game identity

In the editor's Recorded player UUID section:

  1. Search their recorded name or UUID.
  2. Select the correct identity from players observed on this unit's linked servers.
  3. Save the member.

A matching display name is not enough for automatic attendance; it uses the linked UUID. If no identity is available, leave it unlinked and return after the person has been recorded on a linked server. Ask hosting staff to check player detection if recording is unavailable.

Link their panel account

If the member already has a login, a unit administrator can select their Linked panel account and save. This gives the panel an association between the login and the person's name in this unit.

The link does not create a login or grant access. The unit role is assigned separately.

Write a personnel note

  1. Open the member record.
  2. Read existing notes for context.
  3. Add the new note and save.
  4. Confirm it appears with its date and author.

When your account is linked to your own member record in this unit, note authorship uses your unit name. If you use another name in a different unit, that unit's link supplies its own name. Ask an administrator to correct your link if authorship is wrong; do not change the subject member's identity to fix the author.

The member timeline records changes from when history collection began. Older records may have an upgrade baseline rather than a complete earlier history.

Status, archive or delete

Choice Use it when
Active The member is normally expected at tracked events
Reserve Attendance is optional
LOA The member is on leave and excused from attendance expectations; record an optional end date
Former member / archive They have left, but their personnel record should be retained
Permanent deletion An authorized administrator intends to remove the personnel record and its notes/history

Archiving is the normal choice when you want to keep a former member's record. Permanent deletion is a separate administrator action with confirmation. Captured historical event attendance snapshots remain even after a roster record is deleted.

Bulk changes and CSV

Bulk edit… lets permitted staff select members for supported qualification, status or assignment changes. Review the before/after list before confirming. Section moves need enough open billets; insufficient space or a stale roster rejects the batch.

CSV export provides a spreadsheet copy of roster information. Exporting does not create a full panel backup.

5. Assignments and positions

A flat roster needs no organization structure. Units using slots organize people into:

  • Groups: broad containers, such as a platoon or staff organization.
  • Sections: subdivisions, such as HQ, a squad or a mortar section.
  • Billets: actual assignable slots, such as Platoon Sergeant, Vehicle Commander or Company Medic.

Position suggestions are reusable names. A suggestion alone does not create or fill a billet.

To assign someone, use the formation roster or the member editor’s Primary placement field. Each formation can contain named billets and directly assigned members without billets. A person has one primary placement. For an occupied billet, choose Swap placements or leave the displaced member unassigned. Empty billets remain visible; directly assigned members do not consume slots.

Rank and qualifications do not block billet assignments. Use your unit's own staffing policy when deciding whom to place.

Unit administrators create and remove groups, sections and billets in Administration → Structure. Removing organizational items and permanently deleting a member are different actions; read the form's consequences before saving.

6. Qualifications and promotions

Set up rank structures and qualifications

Open Administration → Rank & qualification setup. The two setup screens are separate from Roster → Qualifications, which records awards to people.

  1. Under Qualifications, choose Add qualification. Enter a name, optional abbreviation and description. Names remain stable after creation so existing award records and promotion rules keep their references.
  2. Under Rank structures, choose New rank structure, or select an existing structure. Marine, Medical and Officer structures keep independent rules. Duplicate structure creates an editable copy with its own name.
  3. Add ranks from lowest to highest. Use the up/down controls to change their order; the next rank follows automatically. Existing structures with custom links preserve those links unless you explicitly select Use displayed rank order.
  4. Open Edit requirements & optional paygrade on the destination rank. Enter the days since the previous promotion needed to reach it, then select required qualifications using searchable checkboxes. For Pfc → Cpl, put the time and qualifications on Cpl. Optional paygrade labels do not share rules between structures.
  5. Under Preview eligibility, select a member, a current rank for the simulation and their last promotion date. Choose Explain eligibility to see the next rank, time requirement date and missing qualifications using the unsaved rules. The preview changes no member records.
  6. Save the structure. Saving rules updates eligibility calculations only; staff must approve each actual promotion separately.

Assigned structure/rank names cannot be renamed or removed until roster records are moved off them. Qualifications show who holds them and which ranks require them. Use Edit details / archive to archive a qualification: new awards are blocked, but existing awards and promotion requirements remain valid. Uncheck Archive to restore it. Abbreviations and descriptions can be edited without renaming the qualification.

Each rank also has an optional Rank abbreviation (shown on rosters) field, such as Sergeant → Sgt. Rosters show the abbreviation when set and otherwise use the full rank name. Hover over the roster rank to see the full name. Abbreviations can be changed even when the rank is assigned; full names continue identifying ranks for progression rules. Roster CSV exports include the abbreviation separately.

To permanently remove a qualification, open Qualifications setup → Edit details / archive → Delete qualification. Review affected member awards, promotion requirements, required-reading audiences and document labels, then type its exact name and confirm. Deletion removes active awards and requirements, including former members’ awards; historical change records and published document history remain. Removing a requirement can make someone eligible sooner, but promotions remain manual. Removing the last required-reading audience stops that assignment; it does not assign the document to everyone. Use Archive to preserve existing awards and rules.

Grant or correct a qualification

  1. Open Roster → Qualifications.
  2. Find the member and qualification in the matrix.
  3. Open the cell.
  4. Grant the qualification or correct its date, issuer and notes, then save.

Use search and status/group/section filters to inspect coverage. You can also identify who holds or lacks a qualification. Revocation requires the relevant edit access.

Older qualifications may lack original award dates or issuers. Enter known details rather than inventing them; the timeline separately records your correction. Existing qualification metadata is retained when a bulk grant includes someone who already holds it.

Understand promotion eligibility

The member needs:

  • The correct rank structure and current rank.
  • A configured next rank.
  • A last-promotion date.
  • Enough elapsed time and the next rank's required qualifications.

The panel adds the next rank's time requirement to the last-promotion date. For example, a 30-day requirement after June 1 gives July 1. Reaching that date does not satisfy missing qualifications.

Marine, Medical and Officer paths can have separate rules. An optional paygrade label does not combine their progression.

Review a promotion

  1. Open Roster → Promotions.
  2. Review the member's readiness and missing requirements.
  3. Choose Review promotion when eligible and permitted.
  4. Review the proposed rank/date, enter the required staff note and confirm.

The panel rechecks eligibility and record changes before applying. A stale record needs another review. Promotions are never automatic. Follow the unit's approval policy even when the panel says someone is eligible.

Rank paths, time requirements and qualification names are maintained by permitted administrators in Resources → Rank & qualification setup.

Bulk placement, display options and saved-change collaboration

In Roster → Unassigned, tick members or use Select shown, then Place selected in formation. Choose a destination group, platoon, squad or team and save. Members are placed directly in the formation without filling named billets. The existing member editor and slot controls still handle individual billet assignments.

Formation display options controls callsigns, qualification lists and Reserve/LOA labels beneath names. Display choices and expanded/collapsed formations are saved per browser, account and unit; they do not change other staff’s layout or unit data.

Recent roster changes is a collapsible staff-only feed of member additions, promotions, placements, qualification changes, notes and setup changes. It identifies the author, offers member-record links, and loads older history on request. Members retain read-only roster access and do not see this feed or staff notes.

Saved unit changes sync about every three seconds while the page is visible. Clean rosters and member records refresh automatically. Unsaved editors, active saves and other open dialogs remain intact and display an update notice. Refresh saved changes uses the normal discard guard. Member edits, qualification edits and promotion approvals check the member’s version, allowing staff to edit different members independently; competing edits to the same person must be reopened and reviewed. Multi-member assignments and setup edits still use the unit revision to protect all affected records. This syncs saved changes, not simultaneous typing.

Reusable formation templates

Administrators select a formation in Structure and choose Save as template. Templates store the formation hierarchy and named billets, with no people or callsigns. Formation templates applies a saved layout as a new top-level group or beneath an existing formation. Choose the new formation’s name and destination. Existing layouts and members remain untouched. Deleting a saved template leaves formations previously created from it intact. Templates belong to their unit.

Event end time

Event setup defaults to End date & time in the selected event timezone. Enter the start and end explicitly, including the next date for an overnight event. Set event end using → Duration remains available. Events must last five minutes to 24 hours. With explicit end times, recurring events preserve local start/end clock times; duration mode preserves elapsed minutes. Invalid daylight-saving skipped times are rejected. Editing a recurring series uses the existing occurrence/future-series confirmation and version checks.

7. Events and attendance

Create an event

With event permission:

  1. Open Operations → Events and choose New event.
  2. Select this unit's linked server.
  3. Enter the local start time, explicit timezone and end date/time (or choose Duration).
  4. Enable attendance tracking if attendance should count.
  5. Set recurrence if needed and save.
  6. Review the calendar entry.

Weekly, biweekly and monthly recurrence creates separate occurrences. When editing a series, check whether the action affects one event or the remaining series.

Creating an event does not reserve or automatically start the game server. Coordinate shared-server use with hosting staff.

What automatic attendance counts

A linked UUID observed once on the event's selected server during its tracked event window is enough for Present. Logging in outside an event does not count.

Collection needs a running panel service and a successful live player query. Sampling normally occurs about every 30 seconds; a brief connection between samples may be missed.

The event's membership list follows roster changes until the event starts, then preserves names, UUIDs and expectations. A joined date excludes events before membership.

Membership status is not an attendance outcome

Roster status Event expectation
Active Normally expected
Reserve Optional
LOA Excused

Present, Absent, Excused and Unknown describe event outcomes. They are separate from the person's roster status.

Review, correct and finalize

With attendance permission:

  1. Open the event after it ends.
  2. Inspect UUID evidence and collection gaps.
  3. Review uncertain outcomes. Missing evidence during an outage is not proof of absence.
  4. Save corrections with the required reason.
  5. Finalize when the record is ready.

Finalized records can be reopened with a reason when correction is needed. Cancelled events are excluded from reporting.

Use Operations → Attendance for the monthly member/event grid, server filtering and CSV export. Normal attendance percentages include finalized, expected Present/Absent decisions. Optional Reserve, LOA/Excused, Unknown and pending decisions are excluded.

Member records show recent attendance, but the Attendance page is the place to inspect the unit's monthly results.

8. SOPs, loadouts, awards and campaigns

SOPs and loadouts

Under Resources → SOPs & loadouts, search by category, role or text. Permitted staff can maintain SOP text and external links, or loadouts with an image URL, kit slots, alternatives and notes. Archive obsolete references to retain editing history.

New loadouts start with the standard 24-slot equipment layout, from Helmet through Sidearm. Expand a slot to set its main item, inventory contents and attachments. Each inventory item or attachment supports a quantity and notes. Empty slots can be left unfilled or removed.

Use Slot layout → Custom · start empty to build your own layout, then add and name slots. Choose Save as → Reusable template to keep a staff-only template. When creating a new loadout, select a saved unit template to copy it, fill out the equipment and publish it for members. Templates are independent copies; changing one does not change loadouts already created from it. Switching layouts replaces the current slot contents after confirmation. Editing an existing loadout keeps its original slots.

The unit’s SOP repository includes category filtering, full-text search and sorting by title or update date. Staff and unit administrators can create and edit documents in their own units; panel administrators can access all units.

To create a document:

  1. Choose SOPs → New SOP, then enter a title and category.
  2. Write the procedure in Write SOP, add an external reference link, attach a PDF or Word document, or combine these. Use Heading, Bold, Italic, List and Link to format selected text, and Preview to see the rendered document.
  3. Start a line with #, ## or ### followed by a space to create a section heading. The reader builds a clickable contents sidebar from these headings. Bold, italic, links and uploaded images are rendered; arbitrary HTML is not rendered.
  4. Save, then open the SOP to read it or choose Download text for a .txt copy.

Revision history shows the latest 50 saved changes with the editor’s unit name and time. Choose View revision to read a previous saved version, then Back to current document to return. Viewing history does not change the current SOP; restoring a revision explicitly replaces the working draft. Use Edit reference → Archive this reference to retire a document, and switch the library to Archived to find or restore it. Add images / documents accepts PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), PNG, JPEG and WebP files. Images are inserted at the cursor; PDF and Word attachments download for viewing in your own application. Images are limited to 4 MB, documents to 20 MB, with up to 10 files and 50 MB per save. Each unit can retain 200 MB of attachments, with up to 50 files per document. Files are private to the unit and stored in the panel database, so database backups include them. Pending uploads can be removed before saving. Saved attachments remain available for document history, including after archiving. New revisions record their attachment list; older revisions created before attachment support have no attachments.

Folders, publishing and templates

  • Use Folder path to organize pages, for example Operations/Infantry. The folder sidebar shows the hierarchy and page links. Editing a page’s path moves it; folders appear when pages use them.
  • New SOPs and edits to published SOPs default to Draft. Drafts are visible to staff only. If a document already has a published copy, members continue reading that copy while staff edit. Choose Published when saving to replace the member copy. Existing references are preserved as published during upgrade. Archiving hides a document from members.
  • Choose Template to save a reusable outline for staff. Templates are excluded from member views. In a new SOP, Start from a template offers built-in procedure, communications, training and medical outlines, plus your unit’s saved templates. The template reader also offers Create SOP from template. Template attachments are copied into the new SOP when saved and count toward the unit storage limit. Converting an existing published SOP to Template removes its published copy.
  • In Revision history → View revision, expand Compare with current document to see changed metadata and body lines side by side. Highlighted rows differ at that line number. Restore as draft restores the selected revision’s contents and attachment selection; staff must publish it separately. Saved files remain retained for historical versions.
  • Under Related SOPs, qualifications, positions and events, select relevant items from this unit. Related SOPs open directly from the reader. Qualification, position and event associations appear as reference labels. Members see only published related SOPs and qualification / position labels; staff event records stay private; upcoming briefings and locations appear on My unit.

Awards

Under Roster → Awards, permitted staff maintain the medal/badge catalog and issue awards with a date and citation. These awards are separate from promotion qualifications.

Repeated issues require explicit confirmation. Revocation requires a reason and retains the original issue. Issued names/images are preserved if the catalog later changes.

Campaigns

Under Operations → Campaigns, permitted staff group events using a timezone-aware date window or explicit selection, set a participation threshold and optionally link an award.

Campaign progress uses confirmed Present outcomes against eligible finalized events. Reserve events count here; LOA and Excused are exempt. Unknown is unconfirmed. This differs from the normal attendance percentage.

Complete the campaign and finalize its tracked events before approving member awards. Date-window campaigns must reach their end. The panel does not automatically grant or revoke campaign awards.

Analytics

Under Overview → Analytics, choose a date range and timezone to inspect recorded strength, membership changes, retention and finalized attendance trends. Charts also have table data.

Collection starts when the feature is installed; earlier history is not reconstructed. Rank distribution describes the current roster.

9. Unit administrator tasks

This section is for staff who also hold unit administrator authority.

Accounts & access

  1. Open Administration → Accounts & access → Add account.
  2. Choose Create new login and enter a username and initial password, or use an existing account. Choose Member, Staff or Unit administrator.
  3. Optionally link the account to its member record in this unit.
  4. Save and confirm the assignment.

Panel and unit administrators can create new unit logins through this flow. Give the credentials and the panel’s /login address to the person. Member logins land on My unit and can view their own linked profile. They cannot open other members’ records, staff notes, drafts or server controls. Link the login to a roster member when applicable. Hosting permissions are assigned separately by panel administrators.

Removing unit staff access does not delete the login, remove the roster record or revoke separately assigned server roles. Review those separately when someone leaves.

Structure and catalogs

Create groups, sections and billets in Structure. Maintain independent rank paths, qualifications and position suggestions under Rank & qualification setup. Review the effect of rule changes on existing members' eligibility.

Branding

Use Administration → Customization for the unit description, icon and banner. PNG, JPEG and WebP images up to 4 MB each are supported. Use a square icon and wide banner, preview them and save.

Branding belongs to the unit. Personal color themes remain independent.

Panel administrators handle unit creation and server linking. Unit administration does not by itself grant game-server controls.

Formation builder and Member logins

In Structure, select a formation in the navigator to edit or move it, add smaller formations, or add several billets using Name | count lines. Copy empty layout duplicates the structure without its member assignments. Removing a branch leaves its members unassigned and retains their records. The All members roster remains available for units that do not use formations.

Unit Staff can use Create login beside an active roster member or inside their record. Enter a username and initial password; the panel creates and links a Member account for this unit only. Hand the credentials and the panel’s /login address to the member. This action cannot grant Staff, administrator or hosting permissions. Existing linked accounts are managed through Accounts & access by administrators.

The roster opens on All formations when a structure exists. Group tabs filter the formation tables. Click a member’s name to open their record or click — Open — to fill an empty billet. Members assigned directly appear after the named billets with a dash in the Billet column. Use Manage structure for layout changes, occupied-billet swaps and direct formation assignments.

Member access, dated LOA and reusable billets

Members can view the current roster of their own unit, including names, ranks, callsigns, qualifications and placements. Staff notes, Discord handles, UUIDs, account details, promotion records and former members are excluded from this read-only view. Members cannot change assignments or open staff records.

Staff use Manage assignments to assign members to formations and billets. Only administrators can create, rename, reorder or remove structure items. Unit administrators can use a member record’s Access → Change unit role action to promote a Member to Staff; rank promotion never changes account permissions automatically.

Staff member records have Overview, Qualifications, Attendance, Awards, History, Notes and Access tabs. Qualification details include award dates, instructor and notes. Promotion review remains manual, with an additional next-30-days filter. Unsaved notes are preserved while switching record tabs and guarded when opening another editor.

Select LOA and enter optional start/end dates in Edit member. Dates are inclusive; a blank start begins immediately and a blank end is indefinite. Event expectations use the event’s local date. Members return to Active after the end date; the stored status and history update when the unit roster is opened. Active and past event snapshots stay unchanged. Bulk status changes clear previous LOA dates when the status changes.

Administrators manage saved billet titles through Manage structure → Billet types. Existing slot titles and legacy position labels are carried over. Select a saved title and quantity when adding slots. Set callsigns on individual slots, not on the saved type. An occupant inherits the slot’s callsign unless their member record has a personal override. Moving or unassigning the member updates the inherited callsign. Empty layout copies omit callsigns to avoid duplicating radio identifiers. Removing a saved type preserves existing slots and occupants.

Unit administrators can reset a Member login’s password, disable access to this unit, or restore it through the record’s Access tab. Disabling unit access does not disable other units or hosting permissions. Password resets invalidate existing login sessions. Accounts shared between units, linked to hosting, or holding panel authority require a panel administrator for password resets. Staff/admin memberships are managed through Change unit role or Accounts & access.

Editable demo company

Panel administrators can choose Create demo unit at the top of Units. Confirm Create / open demo to create Demo Company — Training Sandbox. Reopening the action preserves existing demo edits rather than making another copy.

The demo contains 48 fictional members: company HQ, two platoons, four squads and eight teams. Leadership/support personnel hold 24 named billets; 24 troops belong directly to teams, and three billets are vacant. Six published SOPs, six sample kit references, qualification awards, promotion examples, staff notes, callsigns and Active/Reserve/LOA examples are included.

The demo is an ordinary editable unit. It creates no passwords, player UUIDs, game servers or server links. To try Member access, create a login from one of the demo roster records and sign into that account separately. Sample kit references do not apply equipment in game. Keep real personnel and server links in your real units.

Formation rosters are collapsible: companies/platoons start open and deeper formations start closed. Expand all / Collapse all controls affect the current view, and parent-path links open and jump to the parent formation. Search temporarily opens matching branches. Expansion choices are retained while the unit workspace remains open.

Analytics includes an interactive current-rank bar chart. Filter by rank structure, then click a bar to see matching member names and open their staff records. Counts and percentages describe the current roster, independently of the historical date range.

Panel-wide login administration opens on its own Panel users page at /admin/users, accessible from the header to panel administrators. It is independent of the selected unit and never appears below a roster. Unit administrators continue using their unit’s Accounts & access page for local role management.

12. Personnel tools

Search and vacancy overview

Roster search also shows up to 50 matching members across the unit with their full formation path. Choose Go to formation to open that branch. Existing roster view/status filters remain available. Members can use read-only search; staff can open records.

Choose Vacancies for a list of open named billets, their formation paths and any temporary cover. Choose Assign to fill a billet or jump to its formation. A temporary cover does not count as a permanently filled billet.

Backdated promotions and qualification renewal

Promotion review includes an Effective promotion date, defaulting to today. Staff can record an earlier effective date; the panel checks time in rank and recorded award/expiry dates on that date. It cannot predate the previous promotion or be in the future. Approval and a staff note are still required. Editing the member's previous promotion date remains available when entering records from sheets.

Renewal is off by default. A unit administrator can enable Requires renewal for individual qualifications in Rank rules & catalogs. Only enabled qualifications expose expiry controls or renewal tools. Existing explicit expiry dates retain their behavior when upgrading. Expired enabled awards remain in history but do not satisfy promotion requirements after expiry. Disabling renewal makes the qualification permanent for eligibility. Historical dates are retained in the change history.

New member forms require a game name. Recorded UUID choices show when another current or former member already uses that identity; the server also rejects duplicate UUIDs in the same unit. Different units keep independent records.

Compare member records

Open a member record and choose Compare record versions. Select two timeline versions, or compare a version with the current record. Changed fields are highlighted; Include unchanged shows the rest. Fields not captured by an older version are marked Not recorded. Historical comparisons reflect what the panel recorded, not an invented reconstruction of data from before tracking was available.

Temporary cover

Choose Temporary cover on the roster or in a member record. Select a member, a billet and inclusive start/end dates; an empty end means until cancelled. This leaves the member's primary billet or direct formation placement intact. A member or billet cannot have overlapping temporary covers. Active covers appear below the billet's occupant/open slot. Staff can cancel current or upcoming cover. Archiving a member or deleting the covered formation cancels affected cover.

Event formation audiences

Event setup offers Whole unit or Selected formations and their descendants. Selected audiences include primary billet occupants, directly placed troops and dated temporary cover in those branches. Future event rosters follow placement changes; the list freezes at event start. Members see formation-specific events they are invited to. Change upcoming event audiences before deleting a formation they reference.

Meeting agenda

Open Roster → Meeting agenda for promotion reviews, open billets, qualifications expired or expiring within 30 days, and staff follow-ups. Follow-ups can refer to a member or the whole unit, with notes and an optional due date. Edit, complete or reopen them during the meeting. The agenda never approves promotions or changes attendance automatically.

Export a unit backup

Unit administrators can choose Download unit backup from the unit toolbar. The ZIP contains unit-scoped records, catalog, CSV roster, history, notes, event data, documents and attachment files. It includes private staff information, so store it appropriately. Login passwords and game-server binaries are excluded. This is a manual recovery/export archive; there is no restore wizard. Account IDs and linked servers need mapping when moving data to another installation.

Staff with roster editing permission can choose Delete note beside a dated member-record note. Confirm to permanently remove that note; its text is removed and the activity log records who deleted it. Other notes and member history remain.

In Customization, choose an icon or banner and drag its preview to frame the image. Use Zoom to enlarge it or Reset crop to recenter. Arrow keys reposition the image; Shift moves farther. Existing branding can be adjusted without uploading again. Icons save as square images; the banner preview follows the current header shape and may crop differently on other screen sizes. Save branding saves the cropped image; Reset changes discards unsaved adjustments. Source images can be PNG/JPEG/WebP up to 20 MB and 40 megapixels; the panel uploads only the cropped result (up to 4 MB). Keep your original file if you may want a different crop later.

Bulk awards, ordering, editing, printing and event copies

  • Bulk qualifications: Open Bulk edit → Grant qualification. Select members, qualification, award date, instructor and optional expiry, then review and confirm. Existing holders keep their award details; renew those individually in Manage qualifications.
  • Formation order: In Manage structure, select any group, formation or billet and use Move up / Move down to reorder it among its siblings. Child rows also have arrow buttons. Use Edit / move to change its parent. Existing assignments move with the structure.
  • Edit notes: Staff with roster editing access can choose Edit note beside a dated note, change the text, then Save note or Cancel. Original author/date stay visible, with an edited-by name and time. Saving rejects a note changed by another staff member so their edit is not overwritten.
  • Print roster: Choose Print roster, select formation or flat layout, and optionally include callsigns, qualifications and status. It prints all current members and open billets in formation order. Private notes, Discord handles, UUIDs and account details are excluded. Use the browser's print dialog to print or save a PDF.
  • Duplicate event: Open an event and choose Duplicate event. The new-event form copies its briefing, server, event type, location, tracking setting and formation audience, with a new future start time. Choose the intended date/end and save. Attendance, RSVPs, audit history and recurrence are not copied; the original event remains unchanged. A copied event uses the current roster for its new date.

Collaborative editing and workspace layout

The roster keeps collapsible formation tables. Daily actions are in the main toolbar; Manage structure, Open billets and Temporary cover are in the Management row. Bulk actions opens directly to qualification awards/revocations, status changes, placement or unassignment. Member records show an identity header with rank/name, formation, status and callsign, followed by their section tabs. Notes is labelled Staff notes, and History is labelled Service history.

Branding changes no longer invalidate rank edits. Different rank structures or qualification definitions can save from the same starting catalog without overwriting each other. Member A and member B can save independently. Conflicts on the same entry, member or note keep the draft open and reject the stale save. Structural operations and bulk previews retain their revision checks.

Editing status shows other staff working on the same rank structure, qualification, member, note or branding. Roster names can show Editing: labels. This is advisory: editors can still work together, and the API checks conflicts when saving. Presence refreshes periodically and expires within about 45 seconds after a heartbeat stops.

Unit-dialog errors keep entered values and selected files. Copy error details copies the failure message. For a conflict, Copy my draft copies entered fields before reopening the latest record; passwords and security tokens are excluded. Files are represented by their names. For other failures, Try again resubmits the form after you review its contents.

Optional renewals and clearer attendance review

Units with renewable qualifications see Qualifications → Qualification renewal queue. Filter expired or upcoming awards by member and qualification; enabled awards with no expiry date are flagged for review. Select up to 500 awards, set the renewal date, new expiry, instructor and reason, then review and confirm. Existing award notes stay intact. The preview checks only the selected award records, so unrelated work does not invalidate it. If those awards change, return to the renewal details and prepare a new preview. Units with no qualifications configured for renewal do not show the queue or expiry controls.

Event details separate expected members, collector observations, unknown outcomes and staff corrections. Filter the roster to focus on unknown results, missing UUIDs, observations or manual corrections. Collector gaps warn about evidence quality; they do not prove absence. Staff corrections override automatic results and remain in the audit history.
